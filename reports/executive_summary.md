# Executive summary - vulnerability risk at Waalstad Logistics

The current situation at Waalstad Logistics involves 14 systems with 112 findings, including 109 unique CVEs. Out of these, 27 findings are actively exploited, and 18 are related to ransomware. The team has identified 62 open attack paths to crown jewels, which are critical systems. The team currently spends 20 hours per week addressing these issues, and it would take an additional 36 hours per week to fix all exploited findings within two weeks. The team's current approach is to prioritize by CVSS, but this has led to a situation where the crown jewels are expected to be unreachable in 10 weeks, and all exploited findings are expected to be fixed in 11 weeks. The team has identified that by sorting by CVSS, the crown jewels would be unreachable in 3 weeks, and all exploited findings would be fixed in 5 weeks. This shows that sorting by CVSS is not the most effective strategy, as it would allow us to reach our goals faster.

This week, the team will focus on fixing the vulnerabilities in the top library upgrade, which includes the log4j-core component. The team will upgrade this component from version 2.14.1 to 2.25.4, which will fix 7 vulnerabilities. This upgrade is crucial as it will help us reach our goal of having the crown jewels unreachable in 7 weeks and all exploited findings fixed in 6 weeks. This is a significant improvement over the current approach, which would take us 10 weeks to reach the crown jewels and 11 weeks to fix all exploited findings.
