# CRA notification drafts

Product: **waalstad-order-service 2.3.0**  
Rule (Cyber Resilience Act, from 11 Sept 2026): actively exploited vulnerabilities in a product must be reported: early warning within 24 hours, notification within 72 hours, final report within 14 days after a fix is available.

## CVE-2021-44228 in log4j-core 2.14.1

- **Status:** actively exploited (listed in CISA KEV) and used by ransomware
- **Affected component:** log4j-core version 2.14.1 (Maven)
- **Corrective measure:** upgrade to 2.15.0
- **Summary:** Remote code injection in Log4j
- **Deadlines:** early warning within 24 h of becoming aware, notification within 72 h, final report within 14 days after the fix is available
