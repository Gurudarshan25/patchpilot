How it works, and its limits

Score (0 to 100)

Exploitation (max 45: 40 if in CISA KEV, +5 for ransomware, otherwise EPSS percentile x 35) + severity (CVSS x 2) + asset value (criticality x 3, +5 for a crown jewel) + exposure (internet 15, network 7, local 3) + attack-path bonus (up to 15 for bugs that open routes to the crown jewels). P1 means exploited and reachable.

Limitations

  • The company and its scan are fictional. The CVEs and threat data are real, and the scan is enriched with well-known exploited bugs.
  • Fix effort numbers are estimates. Strategies that optimise risk per hour depend on them.
  • The backtest covers 12 later-exploited bugs in its test window, so results can move a few percent with other data.
  • AI explanations are checked for invented numbers and claims, but a human should still review anything sent to management.
